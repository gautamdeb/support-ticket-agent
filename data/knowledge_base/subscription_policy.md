# Subscription & Cancellation Policy

_Last updated: 2026-01-15 · Policy ID: POL-SUB-001_

## Cancelling a subscription

Customers may cancel at any time. Cancellation stops the **next** renewal; it
does not retroactively refund the current period. To cancel:

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Manage Plan**.
2. Select **Cancel Subscription** and confirm.
3. A confirmation email is sent within 5 minutes.

Access to paid features continues until the end of the **current paid period**.

## Downgrades

Downgrading to a lower tier takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle.
The price difference is not prorated or refunded for the current cycle.

## Cancellation + refund

Cancelling does **not** automatically trigger a refund. Any refund is governed
separately by the Refund Policy (POL-REFUND-001) and its 7-day window.

## Auto-renewal

Subscriptions auto-renew by default. Customers can disable auto-renewal from the
same **Manage Plan** screen without cancelling immediately.

## What the agent may do

- Explain how to cancel or disable auto-renewal, quoting the steps above.
- Point customers to the Refund Policy when they ask about money back.
- Escalate disputes about charges already made.
