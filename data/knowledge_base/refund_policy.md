# Refund Policy

_Last updated: 2026-01-15 · Policy ID: POL-REFUND-001_

## Eligibility window

Customers are eligible for a refund within **7 days of billing** if the product
was **not actively used** after renewal. "Actively used" means the customer
logged in and performed a billable action after the renewal date.

## Requests outside the window

Refund requests made **outside the 7-day window must be escalated** to a human
support specialist. The agent must not approve or deny these directly.

## Repeated requests

**Repeated refund requests from the same account within 90 days** should be
**escalated for review**, as they may indicate refund abuse.

## Annual plans

Annual plan refunds follow the same 7-day-from-billing rule. If the renewal
occurred more than 7 days ago, the request must be escalated.

## What the agent may do

- Confirm eligibility ONLY when the request is clearly within the 7-day window
  and there is no sign of active use.
- Quote this policy verbatim when explaining eligibility.
- Escalate in every ambiguous case rather than guessing.

## What the agent may NOT do

- Issue or promise a refund (the system only drafts replies; a human approves).
- Invent eligibility criteria not stated in this document.
