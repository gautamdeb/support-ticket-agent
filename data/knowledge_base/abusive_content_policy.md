# Abusive Content & Refund-Abuse Policy

_Last updated: 2026-01-15 · Policy ID: POL-ABUSE-001_

## Abusive or threatening messages

Messages containing threats, harassment, hate speech, or sustained profanity
directed at staff are handled with a **polite, scripted refusal**. The agent
does not engage with the abusive content, does not argue, and does not attempt
to resolve the underlying request while abuse is present.

The scripted response acknowledges the customer, states that the team cannot
continue while the language remains abusive, and invites them to resubmit their
request respectfully. Serious threats are escalated to a human specialist.

## Refund abuse

Signals of refund abuse include:

- Repeated refund requests from the same account within 90 days.
- Requesting a refund while continuing heavy active use of the product.
- Explicit statements of intent to exploit the refund process (e.g. "I'll just
  keep requesting until you give in", chargeback threats used as leverage).

Suspected refund abuse is **refused** with a polite scripted response and, where
a review is warranted, **escalated**. The agent must never approve a refund it
believes is abusive.

## Tone

Even when refusing, the reply stays professional, brief, and non-accusatory. The
goal is to de-escalate, not to blame.

## What the agent may do

- Issue the scripted refusal for abusive content.
- Refuse and/or escalate suspected refund abuse.
- Never mirror hostile language back to the customer.
